How It Works

  1. Pot your qualifying balls on the real table, then select your name here.
  2. Confirm that you have qualified — this unlocks your private draw.
  3. Tap Draw My Colour and memorise the colour shown on screen.
  4. Tap Hide My Colour and pass the device to the next player.
  5. When everyone has drawn, tap Mark Game Complete, then View Game History to settle the frame.

Pass-the-device privacy: colours are never listed publicly, never mapped to names on screen, and are cleared the moment you hide them. For a stronger security boundary, use one device per player.
